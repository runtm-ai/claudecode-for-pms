Remotion renders React components to video. For a PM that means you can describe an ad variant in code, render twelve versions of it overnight, and ship them to creative testing without going through a design queue.

### When this pays off

- A growth team that wants to test five hooks across three audiences.
- A product launch that needs the same announce in fifteen-second, thirty-second, and sixty-second cuts.
- An onboarding video that has to be re-rendered every time the UI changes.

### The PM job

You're not writing the Remotion code, mostly. You're writing the brief and the variant matrix. The brief lives next to the rest of your PRDs. The variant matrix is a CSV the renderer reads.

### What this is not

It's not "AI video generation". The output is deterministic: same code, same render. The variation comes from data you supply, not from a model improvising. That's what makes it usable for paid media — you can audit every frame.
