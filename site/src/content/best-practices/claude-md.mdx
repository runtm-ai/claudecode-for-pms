`CLAUDE.md` is the file Claude reads automatically every time it opens your repo. If it isn't there, the model invents defaults. If it is there, the model behaves like a teammate who read the wiki.

### What belongs in it

Six sections cover most teams:

- **Product.** What we sell, who buys, what we don't sell.
- **Team.** Roles, who decides what.
- **Stack.** Languages, frameworks, where to look first.
- **Voice.** Banned words, period-end declarative, the headlines that sound like us.
- **Agents and skills.** Pointers to the skills installed in `skills/`.
- **What not to do.** The two or three patterns that have already burned you.

### What does not belong in it

Anything that changes weekly. Roadmap items, sprint goals, current OKRs — those go in `docs/product.md`. The `CLAUDE.md` file should be true in three months without edits.

### Length

Aim for one screen. If it's longer than 200 lines, you have a discoverability problem disguised as a documentation problem. Move the depth to `docs/` and reference it.
