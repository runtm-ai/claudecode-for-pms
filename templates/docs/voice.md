## Principles

1. **Specific nouns.** "Datadog, Salesforce, Stripe" beats "every major tool".
2. **Active verbs.** "Ships the fix" beats "facilitates resolution".
3. **Trust the reader.** Don't over-explain.
4. **Period-end declarative sentences.** No questions. No ellipses for drama.
5. **Plain words.** "Tag" not "invoke". "Run" not "execute". "Build" not "construct".

## Words we don't use

| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| Unlock, empower, leverage | Use, give, let |
| Seamless, frictionless | (describe what happens) |
| Revolutionary, groundbreaking | (cut — show, don't claim) |
| Next-generation, AI-powered | (cut) |
| Ecosystem, platform, solution | The actual product noun |
| Cutting-edge, state-of-the-art | (cut) |
| Robust, scalable, enterprise-grade | (specific capability instead) |

## Tone check

Read the draft aloud. Could you imagine your team saying it on a demo without cringing? If no, simplify.
